Research#

ACMARES 2023

Characterizing the MasterPrint threat on Android devices with capacitive sensors

Miguel Peidro-Paredes, José María de Fuentes, Lorena González-Manzano, Miguel Velasco-Gómez

MasterPrints are real or synthetic fingerprints that match a large share of enrolled users. The paper tests whether GAN-generated MasterPrints work as physical attacks on real Android phones with capacitive sensors.
