Research

Adversarial AI against biometric security.

Doctoral research in AI applied to cybersecurity, UC3M (2022 – 2024, INCIBE-supported), paused to move into industry. The work asked a simple question: can generative models forge a fingerprint that unlocks many real phones?

Characterizing the MasterPrint threat on Android devices with capacitive sensors

M. Peidro-Paredes, J. M. de Fuentes, L. González-Manzano, M. Velasco-Gómez
ARES 2023 · 18th International Conference on Availability, Reliability and Security (ACM)

MasterPrints are real or synthetic fingerprints that match a large share of enrolled users. The paper tests whether GAN-generated MasterPrints work as physical attacks on real Android phones with capacitive sensors.
